Comprehensive Harmful Algal Bloom Monitoring Data Summary — July 2026

The SA Algal Bloom Monitor now ingests data from two comprehensive CSV datasets
containing over 161000 total rows of monitoring data covering all SA coastal
and inland water monitoring sites.

Data coverage:
592 monitoring readings from 624 unique SA sites.
Data spans from June 2025 through July 2026.
780 registered monitoring sites with GPS coordinates across all SA regions.
Regions covered: Metropolitan Adelaide Coorong Southern Fleurieu
Eyre Peninsula Yorke Peninsula Kangaroo Island South East and offshore.

Species tracked:
The platform now monitors ALL harmful algal species not just Karenia.
Key species groups tracked include:
Blue Green Algae Total (Cyanobacteria) — most dangerous freshwater group.
Green Algae Total — includes marine and freshwater species.
Diatoms Total — generally less harmful but tracked for ecosystem health.
Toxin producing BGA Total — subset of BGA that produces dangerous toxins.
Geosmin-MIB producing BGA Total — causes taste and odour in water.
Individual dangerous species: Cylindrospermopsis raciborskii
Nodularia spumigena Microcystis Dolichospermum Aphanizomenon
Raphidiopsis and Karenia species.

Monitoring frequency:
Sites sampled weekly to fortnightly depending on region and risk level.
Coorong sites sampled approximately every 1 to 2 weeks.
Metropolitan Adelaide sites sampled weekly during bloom season.
SASQAP shellfish monitoring sites sampled on regular schedule.

Severity classification used in this platform:
Critical: 50000 or more cells per litre — beach closure recommended.
High: 10000 to 49999 cells per litre — exercise extreme caution.
Medium: 1000 to 9999 cells per litre — exercise caution advised.
Low: below 1000 cells per litre — swimming generally acceptable.
